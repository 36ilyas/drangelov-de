2. General information

Personal data is any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person — for example a name, address, email address or IP address.

As a rule, we only collect and process our users' personal data to the extent necessary to provide a functioning website and our content and services. We do not carry out any tracking, profiling or online marketing.

3. Hosting & server logs

This website is currently provided as a static site via GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When the website is accessed, technically necessary data is transmitted to GitHub's servers and processed in server logs — typically:

  • IP address of the accessing device
  • Date and time of access
  • URL accessed and HTTP status code
  • User agent (browser and operating system identifier)
  • Amount of data transferred

The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in the secure and reliable operation of the website). As GitHub has its headquarters in the USA, data may be transferred to a third country; GitHub provides appropriate safeguards for this (including the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses). Further information can be found in the GitHub privacy statement.

4. Embedded services & third-party content

Our website loads some content and fonts from external providers. When these are accessed, your IP address is transmitted to the respective provider, because your browser loads the content directly from there.

Google Fonts

We use the "Inter" and "Lora" fonts via Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). When the website is loaded, the fonts are retrieved from Google's server. In doing so, your IP address is transmitted to Google. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (consistent and appealing presentation of the website).
More information: < href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" rel="noopener" target="_blank">policies.google.com/privacy.

Unsplash

Some images are delivered via the Unsplash CDN (images.unsplash.com; Unsplash Inc., USA). When they are accessed, your IP address is transmitted to Unsplash. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (presentation of image material).
More information: < href="https://unsplash.com/privacy" rel="noopener" target="_blank">unsplash.com/privacy.

Google Maps

On the home page, in the "Contact & directions" section, a map is embedded via Google Maps (Google Ireland Limited). For data protection reasons, the map is only loaded once you actively click "Load map" (known as click-to-load). Only then is the following data transmitted to Google:

  • Your IP address
  • URL of the page visited (referrer)
  • Information about your browser and device
  • Location data, if you have enabled this in your browser

Google may process this data in the USA. We have no influence over the processing carried out by Google. Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (provision of convenient directions).
More information: < href="https://policies.google.com/privacy" rel="noopener" target="_blank">policies.google.com/privacy.

As long as you do not actively load the map, none of this data is transmitted to Google.

Online medical form

On the "Complete your medical form online" page, you can fill in our patient questionnaire directly in your browser. This processing takes place exclusively locally on your device: your entries are not transmitted to us or any third parties and are not stored. When you close or reload the page, the entries are lost. Only when you choose to download the form as a PDF – and any email you send yourself – does the data leave your device.

5. Cookies

This website itself sets no cookies of its own and uses neither tracking nor marketing cookies. However, some embedded third-party services (e.g. Google Maps) may set their own cookies — please refer to the linked privacy notices of the respective providers.

6. Your rights as a data subject

At any time, you have the following rights towards us regarding your personal data:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Rectification of inaccurate data (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten", Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Objection to processing (Art. 21 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, an informal message to info@drangelov.de is sufficient.
